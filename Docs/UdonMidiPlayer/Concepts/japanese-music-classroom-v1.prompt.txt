Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: First environment concept painting for a Unity VRChat MIDI player demo; a buildable Japanese school music classroom.
Primary request: Design a beautiful, inviting Japanese school music classroom around one existing white grand piano, prioritizing piano listening and playing. Single cohesive wide interior illustration, landscape 16:9.
Scene/backdrop: A believable rectangular classroom approximately 10 m wide by 8 m deep, 3.4 m ceiling. Tall repeated aluminum-frame windows along the left exterior wall, soft ivory curtains, warm oak parquet floor, cream plaster above warm wood wainscoting. Front wall has a dark muted green music chalkboard with faint staff lines, a simple wall clock, understated storage cabinetry. Right wall has a glazed school sliding door opening to a quiet corridor and a modest noticeboard. Outside the windows are soft green trees and a distant school building, secondary and low detail. A few ordinary wooden music-classroom chairs placed along the rear/right perimeter; the center stays open and easy to walk through.
Subject: One elegant white grand piano with its lid propped open, readable piano anatomy, exposed warm golden strings, 88-key keyboard facing the viewer, matching white piano bench and brass pedals. Place it in the front half of the room with generous clearance around it. Beside the piano, one restrained compact dark control panel at standing height, subtle cyan accents and a few simple interface marks; secondary to the piano. Above the keyboard only, a delicate sparse vertical cascade of translucent pale blue crystal MIDI note bars provides a restrained signature visual, no giant display wall.
Style/medium: High quality Japanese animation background art meeting polished stylized 3D game environment concept; precise clean architecture, painterly controlled surfaces, clear modelable silhouettes, realistic human-scale proportions, soft physically believable shading. Architectural environment art, not a photo, not a UI screenshot.
Composition/framing: Single uninterrupted image, eye-height wide 28mm interior perspective from the rear right of the classroom looking diagonally toward the piano and front chalkboard, showing floor, ceiling, windows and right door. The piano is the focal point in the middle distance. Spacious foreground circulation; at least a 1.2m clear approach to the keyboard and a clear route from the door to the controls. No distracting extreme lens distortion.
Lighting/mood: Peaceful after-school golden afternoon light streaming through the side windows, long soft window-frame shadows across the wood floor, cool soft skylight fill, warm lived-in calm. Preserve details in highlights and corners; gentle bloom only. Thin curtains softly catch the light.
Color palette: Natural honey wood, cream, muted chalkboard green, white piano, subtle sky blue accents.
Constraints: No people. Exactly one piano and one bench. No other instruments, no ensemble arrangement, no drum kit, no orchestra, no band stage. No neon nightclub lighting, no giant holograms, no ornamental floating music symbols, no logos, no watermark, no captions, no concept-sheet border, no split panels. Music classroom identity must be clear and suitable for a practical modular Unity scene.
